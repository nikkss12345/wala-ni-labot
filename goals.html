My Future Goals

As a BSIT student, I want to build a strong foundation in information technology. I know that reaching my goals will take time, effort, and consistent learning.

My goals

  • Improve my programming and problem-solving skills.
  • Learn how to create useful websites and applications.
  • Complete my studies and become a responsible graduate.
  • Build a portfolio of projects that shows what I can do.
  • Find a career where I can use technology to help solve real problems.

My goal is not to know everything immediately. It is to keep learning and make progress every day.
